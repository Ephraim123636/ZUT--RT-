01 — THE PROBLEM

Making the results process easier to understand.

Students may experience delays and uncertainty when accessing examination results because results pass through different processing stages before publication.

⏳

Waiting & Uncertainty

Students may not know whether their results have been submitted, processed, approved or published.

🔎

Limited Visibility

Without a centralized tracking point, it can be difficult to know the current stage of the results process.

🏢

Unnecessary Enquiries

Students may need to make repeated enquiries when they simply want to know whether results are available.

Who is affected?

Students Lecturers Departments Results/Exams Staff Administrators

02 — OUR SOLUTION

Introducing CAT ZUT Results Tracker.

ZUT-RT is a proposed centralized platform where authorized staff can manage results while students can conveniently view results that have been published.

03 — KEY FEATURES

Designed around the results journey.

The proposed platform focuses on clear access, organized processing and visibility of results publication.

🔐

Secure Login

Role-based access for students and authorized staff.

📤

Results Submission

Authorized staff can submit results through a structured process.

🔎

Results Tracking

Track stages such as submitted, reviewed, approved and published.

📢

Publication

Approved results can be published for student access.

📊

Results Viewing

Students can view results that have been published to them.

🔔

Notifications

Students can be informed when results become available.

04 — BENEFITS

What ZUT-RT aims to improve.

Our objectives

  1. Provide convenient access to published student results.
  2. Provide authorized staff with a centralized results platform.
  3. Improve visibility of the results publication process.
  4. Reduce unnecessary enquiries about result availability.
  5. Provide a structured results submission and publication process.
  6. Improve communication between results administrators and students.
01

For Students

Convenient access, clearer status information and less uncertainty.

02

For Staff

Structured submission, monitoring and management of results.

03

For the Institution

A more organized approach to results publication and communication.
